# ADR-005: Repairs are reviewable proposals

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

Silent self-healing hides real problems: a test that quietly clicks something else keeps passing. A runtime heal also lived only in a local cache, so it vanished on a fresh machine and could not be reviewed.

## Decision

Every heal and every abstention is a **repair** stored in `repairs.json` beside the test.

- A heal is recorded only after verification (outcome, then recorded effect). A heal that fails its check fails the step and is not recorded.
- Heals start as `proposed`. A person accepts (the selector is folded into `grounded.json`) or rejects (the selector is never proposed again for that step).
- A step with no trustworthy match abstains and becomes `needed`.
- Agents can read repairs and submit a patched spec; they cannot accept or reject heals.
- `gimbal test` exits 2 when repairs are waiting; `--strict` makes that a failure.

## Consequences

- Healing is visible in review and in CI, and survives deleting the cache.
- There is a decision to make after each heal. In exchange, a wrong heal cannot hide.
